About Zorawar

A name that
means strength.

Zorawar arrived on a quiet January morning and immediately made everything louder, warmer, and more meaningful. His name — rooted in the Punjabi and Sanskrit tradition — means mighty, powerful, brave. We think it suits him perfectly.

He came into this world at 3.26 kg, small but mighty, and every single day he proves it. We document his journey here — the feeds, the milestones, the 3am moments, and every tiny victory — because these days are too precious not to keep.

A note for Zorawar

"You are the reason our hearts learned to hold more than we ever thought possible."

Dear Zorawar — you came into this world with the quietest entrance and made the biggest noise in our hearts. We watch you sleep and wonder how we ever lived without the sound of your breathing. We feed you every few hours through the night and somehow, not one moment feels like a sacrifice. You are our greatest adventure, our most important work, and our deepest joy — all in one tiny, perfect person. This website is our gift to you: proof that every moment mattered.

— Mama & Papa, always
